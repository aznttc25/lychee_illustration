Hi, I’m Sarah Ly.

This is Lychee Illustration. I’m a Los Angeles–based illustrator creating work centered around storytelling, environments, and food. Through my illustrations, I explore how everyday moments and personal memories can be translated into visual narratives.

Working across traditional, digital, and motion mediums, my practice focuses on creating thoughtful and atmospheric imagery that connects with viewers. Outside of my studio practice, I enjoy hiking, walking along the beach, and never missing a sunset.

Currently open to internships, freelance projects, and commissions.
